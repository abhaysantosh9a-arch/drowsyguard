An AI that watches your eyes through a camera and sounds an alarm the moment they stay closed too long while driving.

Try the live demo

How it works

👁️

1. Detect

A face-landmark AI finds six points around each eye in real time, right in your browser.

📐

2. Measure

It computes the Eye Aspect Ratio (eye height ÷ width). The value drops toward zero as the eye closes.

🚨

3. Alert

If both eyes stay closed longer than your set time, a loud alarm plays. Normal blinks are ignored.

For best results

Place the camera at dashboard height facing you, in good light. Sunglasses, glare and very dark cabins reduce accuracy; an infrared camera works better at night. This is a safety aid, not a guarantee. If you feel drowsy, pull over and rest.
